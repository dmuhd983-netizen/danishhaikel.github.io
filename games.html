Classroom Games

Bilingual games on the United Nations, arms control and counter-terrorism, built for the UniSZA SULAM programme with secondary school students.

Permainan dwibahasa tentang Pertubuhan Bangsa-Bangsa Bersatu, kawalan senjata dan pembanterasan keganasan, dibina untuk program SULAM UniSZA bersama pelajar sekolah menengah.

Hand gesture quiz

Kunci Petir PBB UN Lightning Lock

Point at an answer with your finger to lock it, then make a fist to strike. Covers the UN, the Security Council, the veto and the Council's toolkit. Halakan jari ke jawapan untuk mengunci, kemudian genggam tangan untuk melancarkan petir. Merangkumi PBB, Majlis Keselamatan, kuasa veto dan alat Majlis.

Mission to Protect the World Kuiz Misi Menjaga Dunia
