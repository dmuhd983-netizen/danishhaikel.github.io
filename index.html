About

An International Relations student who reads the world through its resources.

I study International Relations at Universiti Sultan Zainal Abidin (UniSZA), in the Faculty of Law and International Relations. My research centres on critical minerals governance in Malaysia and the wider politics of energy in the region.

Through Sempadan Fikir, I turn that research into clear, visual commentary on the questions that matter for Malaysia: who controls oil and gas, what federalism means for Sabah and Sarawak, and how rare earths and nickel are redrawing the regional map.
